Zoroastrianism

One of the world's oldest monotheistic religions, Zoroastrianism originated in ancient Iran and profoundly influenced later religious traditions. Its teachings on cosmic dualism, the battle between good and evil, and the ultimate triumph of good have shaped philosophical and religious thought for millennia.

👑

Sasanian Empire

The Sasanian Persian Empire (224-651 CE) was one of the most significant empires in human history. Known for its sophisticated administration, cultural achievements, and military prowess, it played a crucial role in the development of Persian civilization and international trade.

📖

Avesta

The Avesta is the sacred text of Zoroastrianism, containing hymns, prayers, and legal passages attributed to the prophet Zoroaster. As one of the oldest known religious texts, it provides invaluable insights into ancient Iranian religion, society, and language.

✍️

Middle Persian Corpus

The Middle Persian (Pahlavi) corpus represents the linguistic and literary heritage of the Sasanian period. These texts, written in various scripts, preserve important religious, administrative, and literary works that illuminate the intellectual and cultural life of ancient Iran.

📚

Shahname

The Shahname (Book of Kings), composed by Firdausi in the 10th century, is the world's longest epic poem. It chronicles the mythological and historical kings of Persia, preserving ancient Persian legends, heroic tales, and cultural values in magnificent poetic form.
